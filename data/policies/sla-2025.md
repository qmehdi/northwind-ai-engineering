# Service Level Agreement

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) describes the uptime commitments, measurement methodology, exclusions, service credits, and support response time targets that Northwind Cloud provides to customers on the Starter, Pro, and Enterprise plans. This document is effective 2025-03-01 and supersedes sla-2023.

## Uptime Commitments

Northwind Cloud commits to the following monthly uptime percentages for the production environment of each plan:

| Plan | Monthly Uptime Commitment |
|------------|---------------------------|
| Starter | 99.5 percent |
| Pro | 99.9 percent |
| Enterprise | 99.95 percent |

Uptime is calculated as a percentage of total minutes in a calendar month during which the core service is available and responsive, excluding minutes attributable to the exclusions listed below. Availability is measured using Northwind Cloud's internal monitoring systems, which sample core API and dashboard endpoints at 60 second intervals from three geographically distributed regions. A minute is counted as downtime only if at least two of the three monitoring regions report a failed check in that minute.

## Measurement and Reporting

1. Uptime is calculated on a calendar month basis, from the first to the last day of the month.
2. Customers may request a copy of the monthly uptime report for their account by contacting support; reports are retained for 12 months.
3. Uptime figures are rounded to two decimal places for the purpose of determining service credit eligibility.
4. Disputes over measurement data must be raised within 30 days of the end of the month in question, accompanied by supporting evidence such as timestamps and error logs.

## Exclusions

The following are excluded from downtime calculations and do not count against the uptime commitments above:

1. Scheduled maintenance windows, provided Northwind Cloud gives at least 48 hours advance notice via email or the status page.
2. Emergency maintenance required to address a security vulnerability, applied with as much notice as reasonably possible.
3. Outages caused by factors outside Northwind Cloud's reasonable control, including third party network or infrastructure failures, denial of service attacks, and force majeure events.
4. Issues caused by the customer's own equipment, configuration, or misuse of the service, including exceeding documented rate limits.
5. Downtime occurring during a free trial period or on non-production or sandbox environments.

## Service Credits

If actual monthly uptime falls below the applicable commitment, customers are eligible for a service credit applied to the following month's invoice. Credits are calculated as a percentage of the monthly fee for the affected plan, based on the shortfall band below:

| Uptime Shortfall Below Commitment | Service Credit |
|-----------------------------------|-----------------|
| Up to 1.0 percentage point | 5 percent of monthly fee |
| 1.01 to 3.0 percentage points | 10 percent of monthly fee |
| More than 3.0 percentage points | 25 percent of monthly fee |

Service credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the uptime commitments. Total credits issued in any single month will not exceed 25 percent of that month's fee for the affected plan. Credits have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for a refund.

## Claiming a Service Credit

1. Customers must submit a service credit request within 30 days of the end of the month in which the shortfall occurred.
2. Requests must be submitted through the support portal or by emailing the account's designated support contact, including the account name, affected plan, and the month in question.
3. Northwind Cloud will review the request against internal monitoring data and respond within 10 business days with a determination.
4. Approved credits are applied automatically to the next billing cycle; they are not issued as separate refunds.
5. Requests submitted after the 30 day window will not be honored.

## Support Response Time Targets

Support requests are triaged by priority level. Response time targets, measured from the time a ticket is submitted through the support portal to the time a qualified engineer provides an initial substantive response, are as follows:

| Priority | Description | Enterprise | Pro | Starter |
|----------|--------------------------------------------|------------|---------|---------|
| P0 | Critical: service unavailable or data loss | 15 minutes | 1 hour | 4 hours |
| P1 | High: major function impaired | 1 hour | 4 hours | 8 hours |
| P2 | Medium: minor function impaired | 8 hours | 24 hours| 48 hours|
| P3 | Low: general question or minor issue | 2 business days | 3 business days | 5 business days |

P0 and P1 tickets may be submitted at any time and are monitored 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. P2 and P3 tickets are handled during standard business hours, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays observed by Northwind Cloud. Response time targets reflect time to first response and do not represent a commitment to full resolution within the stated window; resolution time depends on the complexity of the issue.

## General Terms

1. This SLA applies only to accounts in good standing with no overdue invoices.
2. Northwind Cloud reserves the right to update this SLA with at least 30 days notice to customers, except where changes are required for legal or security reasons.
3. Nothing in this SLA limits any other rights or remedies available under the customer's master subscription agreement with Northwind Cloud.
