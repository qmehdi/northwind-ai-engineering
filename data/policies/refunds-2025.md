# Refunds and Billing Disputes

## Overview

This policy explains when Northwind Cloud issues refunds, how customers raise billing disputes, and how chargebacks are handled. It applies to all customers on monthly plans and annual plans, and it replaces the refunds-2024 policy in full as of the effective date above. Finance and Support jointly manage refund decisions to keep processing consistent across all accounts.

## Refund Eligibility

1. Duplicate charges: if the same invoice or subscription fee is charged more than once due to a system or processing error, Northwind Cloud refunds the duplicate amount in full within 5 business days of confirmation.
2. Annual plans: customers on annual plans may request a pro rata refund within 30 days of their renewal date. The refund amount is calculated based on the unused portion of the annual term remaining after cancellation.
3. Monthly plans: fees paid on monthly plans are not refundable, except where an outage covered by the Service Level Agreement (SLA) results in SLA credits. SLA credits are applied to the next invoice rather than issued as cash refunds, unless the account is being closed, in which case the credit value is refunded instead.
4. Add on services, one time setup fees, and usage overages are evaluated case by case and are not automatically covered by the eligibility rules above.

## How to Request a Refund or Raise a Dispute

1. Submit a request through the billing section of the customer portal or by emailing billing support, including the invoice number, the amount in question, and a short description of the issue.
2. Requests related to duplicate charges should be submitted as soon as the duplicate charge is noticed, since the 5 business day refund window begins once Northwind Cloud confirms the duplicate.
3. Requests for annual plan pro rata refunds must be submitted within the 30 day window following renewal. Requests received after this window are not eligible under this policy.
4. Disputes related to SLA credits should reference the specific outage or incident, and are reviewed against the SLA terms in the customer's service agreement.
5. Northwind Cloud acknowledges all refund and dispute requests within 2 business days of receipt and provides a decision or a status update within 10 business days.

## Chargeback Handling

1. If a customer initiates a chargeback with their card issuer or bank instead of contacting Northwind Cloud directly, the account is flagged for review and access to paid features may be paused until the chargeback is resolved.
2. Northwind Cloud responds to chargeback notifications from payment processors within the timeframe set by the processor, typically providing invoice records, usage logs, and correspondence as evidence.
3. Customers are encouraged to raise disputes directly with Northwind Cloud before filing a chargeback, since direct resolution is faster and avoids a temporary hold on the account.
4. If a chargeback is found to be invalid after review, the disputed amount remains due and the account may be subject to standard collections procedures.

## Approval and Processing

1. Refunds up to and including 2,000 USD are approved by the Support team lead handling the request.
2. Refunds above 2,000 USD require Finance approval before processing. These requests are routed automatically to Finance once the amount is confirmed, and customers are notified that additional review is in progress.
3. Once approved, refunds are processed and issued within 5 business days for duplicate charges, and within 10 business days for annual plan pro rata refunds and other approved cases.
4. All approved refunds are recorded against the original invoice for audit purposes, and a confirmation is sent to the billing contact on file.

## Payment Method and Currency

1. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used for the charge, whether that is a credit card, bank transfer, or another supported method.
2. If the original payment method is no longer valid or active, the customer's billing contact is contacted to arrange an alternative refund method, which may extend processing time beyond the standard windows above.
3. Refunds are issued in the same currency as the original charge. All dollar thresholds in this policy, including the 2,000 USD approval limit, are stated in United States dollars and converted at the prevailing rate for accounts billed in other currencies.

## Contact and Escalation

1. Customers should direct all refund and billing dispute requests to billing support through the customer portal or the billing support email address listed in their account settings.
2. If a customer disagrees with a refund decision, they may request escalation to a Finance reviewer, who reviews the case and responds within 10 business days of the escalation request.
3. This policy is reviewed periodically and may be updated; the effective date at the top of this document reflects the version currently in force.
