# Refunds and Billing Disputes

This policy explains how Northwind Cloud handles refund requests, billing disputes, and related approvals for customers on annual subscription plans. It applies to all Northwind Cloud accounts billed on an annual basis, including Starter, Growth, and Enterprise tiers, effective 2024-02-01.

## 1. Eligibility for Refunds

1. Refunds are available only for annual plans.
2. A refund request must be submitted within 14 days of the renewal date for the request to be considered.
3. Requests submitted after the 14 day window will not be eligible for a refund, regardless of usage level.
4. Monthly plans are not eligible for refunds under this policy, as they can be cancelled at the end of the current billing cycle instead.
5. Refunds apply to the unused portion of the annual subscription fee only and do not include one time setup fees, custom integration work, or professional services charges.

## 2. Approval Requirements

1. Refund requests of 500 USD or less may be processed directly by the billing support team once eligibility is confirmed.
2. Refund requests above 500 USD require review and approval from the Finance team before any funds are released.
3. Finance approval is based on account standing, prior refund history, and confirmation that the request falls within the 14 day renewal window.
4. Customers will be notified by email once a decision has been made, whether the request is approved, denied, or requires additional information.

## 3. How to Submit a Dispute

1. All billing disputes and refund requests must be submitted by email only. Phone requests, chat messages, and support tickets submitted through the in app help widget will not be accepted as a substitute for the email process.
2. The email must include the account name, the invoice number in question, and a brief description of the issue.
3. Disputes should be sent to the billing support address associated with your account. Your account representative can confirm this address if you are unsure where to send your request.
4. Northwind Cloud aims to acknowledge receipt of a billing dispute email within 2 business days of submission.

## 4. Review and Resolution Timeline

| Refund Amount | Reviewing Party | Typical Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| 500 USD or less | Billing Support | 3 to 5 business days |
| Above 500 USD | Finance Team | 7 to 10 business days |

1. Response times begin once the email dispute has been acknowledged as received.
2. If additional documentation is needed, such as proof of duplicate charges or an internal approval record, the review clock is paused until the requested information is provided.
3. Once a refund is approved, funds are returned to the original payment method used at the time of purchase. Refunds are not issued as account credit unless the customer specifically requests this option in writing.

## 5. Non Refundable Charges

1. Usage based overage charges billed within the current annual term are not refundable, since these reflect services already consumed.
2. Add on modules purchased separately from the base annual plan follow the same 14 day renewal window, measured from the add on's own renewal date rather than the base plan renewal date.
3. Any discounts or promotional credits applied to the annual plan will be recalculated and deducted from the refund amount if the refund would otherwise cause the account to fall below the standard pricing tier for the services used.

## 6. Disputing an Unauthorized Charge

1. If a customer believes a charge was made in error or without authorization, this should also be reported by email only, following the same process outlined in Section 3.
2. Unauthorized charge disputes are prioritized ahead of standard refund requests and are typically acknowledged within 2 business days.
3. Customers are encouraged to review their invoices promptly after each annual renewal, since the 14 day window for refund eligibility applies equally to disputes involving unauthorized or unexpected charges.
4. If Northwind Cloud confirms that a charge was made in error, the correction will be processed as a refund following the approval thresholds described in Section 2, based on the amount involved.

## 7. Account Standing During a Dispute

1. Submitting a billing dispute does not pause or suspend access to the Northwind Cloud platform. Services continue as normal while the review is underway.
2. Customers should continue to pay any undisputed portion of an invoice on time to avoid service interruption, even if another portion of the same invoice is under review.
3. If a dispute is resolved in the customer's favor, any applicable refund will be processed according to the timelines in Section 4, and the account will not be penalized for the disputed amount during the review period.

For questions about this policy or help identifying the correct email address for your account, contact your Northwind Cloud account representative before submitting a formal dispute.
