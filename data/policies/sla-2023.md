# Service Level Agreement

This Service Level Agreement (SLA) describes the uptime commitments, service credits, and incident response times that Northwind Cloud provides to customers on the Starter, Pro, and Enterprise plans. It applies to all production subscriptions and takes effect for billing periods beginning on 2023-01-15.

## Uptime Commitment

Northwind Cloud commits to the following monthly uptime percentages, measured over each calendar month for the core platform API and dashboard:

| Plan | Monthly Uptime Commitment |
|------|---------------------------|
| Starter | 99.0 percent |
| Pro | 99.5 percent |
| Enterprise | 99.9 percent |

Uptime is calculated as total minutes in the month minus downtime minutes, divided by total minutes in the month, expressed as a percentage. Scheduled maintenance windows, communicated at least 48 hours in advance, are excluded from downtime calculations.

## Service Credits

If actual uptime in a given month falls below the applicable commitment, the customer is eligible for a service credit applied to the next invoice. Only two credit tiers are available under this SLA:

1. 5 percent of the monthly subscription fee, if uptime falls below the plan's commitment but remains at or above 98.0 percent.
2. 10 percent of the monthly subscription fee, if uptime falls below 98.0 percent.

Service credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for any failure to meet the uptime commitments described above. Credits do not carry over between billing periods and cannot be redeemed for cash.

## Incident Response Times

Northwind Cloud classifies incidents by severity, with P0 representing a full service outage or critical data integrity issue. Response time is measured from the moment a support ticket is opened and confirmed by our support system to the moment a qualified engineer acknowledges the ticket.

| Plan | P0 Response Time |
|------|-------------------|
| Enterprise | 30 minutes |
| Pro | 2 hours |
| Starter | 8 hours |

For incidents classified below P0 (P1, P2, and P3), response times are documented separately in the Support Handbook and are not covered by the credit structure in this SLA. All plans include 24x7 monitoring, but ticket intake hours vary by plan tier as described in your support onboarding materials.

## Claims Process

To request a service credit, the customer must submit a claim through the Northwind Cloud support portal within 15 days of the end of the affected billing month. Claims submitted after this window will not be honored. Each claim must include:

1. The account or subscription identifier.
2. The dates and approximate times of the outage or degraded service.
3. Any incident ticket numbers already on file with support.

Northwind Cloud will review the claim against internal monitoring records and respond with a determination within 10 business days of receipt. If the claim is approved, the credit will be reflected on the next regularly scheduled invoice.

## Exclusions

The uptime commitments and service credits described in this SLA do not apply to downtime or degraded performance caused by:

1. Scheduled maintenance windows communicated in advance.
2. Factors outside Northwind Cloud's reasonable control, including third-party network outages or force majeure events.
3. Customer-side misconfiguration, including incorrect API usage or exceeding documented rate limits.
4. Beta or preview features that are explicitly labeled as not covered by this SLA.

## Definitions and Measurement

Downtime is defined as any period during which the core platform API returns error responses for more than 5 consecutive minutes across a statistically significant sample of requests, as measured by Northwind Cloud's internal monitoring systems. Partial degradation affecting a single feature, rather than the core API or dashboard, is not counted toward the uptime calculation unless it persists for more than 60 consecutive minutes.

This SLA applies per subscription and is calculated independently for each customer account. It does not apply to sandbox, trial, or non-production environments. Questions about this SLA, including plan-specific clarifications, should be directed to the account's assigned support contact or submitted through the support portal referenced in the Claims Process section above.
