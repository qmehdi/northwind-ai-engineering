# Support Hours and Channels

This document describes when and how customers can reach Northwind Cloud support, depending on subscription plan, issue severity, and preferred channel. It applies to all customers on the Starter, Pro, and Enterprise plans.

## Support Plans and Hours

1. Starter: support is available during business hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 in the customer's local time zone.
2. Pro: support is available during extended hours, Monday to Saturday, 7:00 to 21:00 in the customer's local time zone.
3. Enterprise: support is available 24x7 for issues classified as P0 or P1. Issues classified as P2 or P3 on the Enterprise plan are handled during business hours, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00.

Severity levels are defined as follows:

| Severity | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| P0 | Critical outage, service unusable for most users |
| P1 | Major functionality degraded for many users |
| P2 | Minor issue affecting a subset of users |
| P3 | General question or minor cosmetic issue |

## Support Channels

1. Portal: all plans can submit and track tickets through the Northwind Support Portal at any time. This is the primary channel for all severities.
2. Email: all plans can email support at support@northwindcloud.com. Email tickets are automatically logged in the portal.
3. Phone: phone support is available only to Enterprise plan customers for P0 issues, through the dedicated Enterprise emergency line. Phone requests for P1, P2, or P3 issues should be submitted through the portal or email instead, so they can be properly tracked and routed.

Customers on Starter and Pro plans do not have access to phone support and should use the portal or email for all requests.

## Response Time Targets

While this document focuses on hours and channels, the following response time targets apply within each plan's covered hours, to help set expectations:

| Plan | P0 | P1 | P2 | P3 |
|------|----|----|----|----|
| Starter | 4 hours | 8 hours | 2 business days | 3 business days |
| Pro | 2 hours | 4 hours | 1 business day | 2 business days |
| Enterprise | 30 minutes | 1 hour | 4 hours | 1 business day |

For Enterprise, the P0 and P1 targets apply at all hours, consistent with 24x7 coverage. For Starter and Pro, targets apply only within each plan's stated support hours.

## Language Support

1. English is fully supported across all plans, channels, and hours described above.
2. German is supported on a best effort basis, subject to agent availability at the time the ticket is submitted. Customers requesting German language support should note this in the ticket subject line, so it can be routed to an available German speaking agent when possible.
3. All written documentation, portal fields, and automated notifications are provided in English.

## Holiday Coverage

1. On Northwind Cloud designated holidays, staffing is reduced across all plans and channels.
2. Enterprise customers continue to receive 24x7 coverage for P0 and P1 issues on holidays, consistent with standard Enterprise hours.
3. Starter and Pro support hours are suspended on designated holidays; tickets submitted during this time are queued and addressed on the next business day.
4. The current list of designated holidays and any temporary changes to coverage are posted on the Northwind Cloud status page in advance.

## What to Include in a Ticket for Fastest Resolution

To help support agents resolve issues as quickly as possible within the response targets above, include the following in every ticket:

1. Account name and the environment affected, such as production, staging, or sandbox.
2. A clear description of the issue, including what you expected to happen and what actually happened.
3. Steps to reproduce the issue, in order.
4. Timestamps of when the issue occurred, including time zone.
5. Any error messages, error codes, or screenshots.
6. The number of users or accounts impacted, and whether the impact is ongoing.
7. The severity you believe applies, using the P0 to P3 scale above, so the ticket can be routed correctly from the start.

Tickets submitted through the portal with this information attached are typically resolved faster than tickets submitted with partial detail, since agents do not need to request follow up information before beginning investigation.
